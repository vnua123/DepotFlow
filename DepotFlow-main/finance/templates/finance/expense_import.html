How the sheet is read

  • Cell A of the heading row says Date; the headings to its right are the expense categories.
  • Each day is a row. Every amount becomes one expense for that day and category.
  • Total rows, empty cells and zeros are ignored. Columns after the last category heading are ignored.
  • Nothing is saved until you confirm on the next page.
  • Uploading an updated sheet is safe: amounts already in the app are skipped, and changed amounts are listed for you to deal with.
