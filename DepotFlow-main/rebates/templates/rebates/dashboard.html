Only the Commercial Manager can set reinvestment targets. Expected rebates come from existing monthly segment calculations; actual rebates come from NBC rebate receipts recorded in Finance. Reinvestment is assessed as the positive change in total working capital from the profit month-end health check to the following month-end health check. A depot is flagged when net profit is zero/negative or that increase is below the required percentage of net profit. A missing profit-month or following-month health check is shown as pending.
